Compression Multi-Step:

  1. Sorting: Sort by name_id enables monotonic delta encoding.
  2. Delta Encoding: $\Delta \text{id} = \text{id}_i - \text{id}_{i-1} \Rightarrow$ small integers.
  3. LEB128 Varint: Delivers 1–2 bytes for $\Delta$, type, and zigzag offset.

Bloom Filter Rejection:

$$P(\text{False Positive}) \approx \left(1 - e^{-k \cdot n / m}\right)^k$$

With $m=64$ bits, $k=3$ hashes, and $n \le 8$ symbols per scope, false positive rate is < 2.5%. Over 97% of cold blocks are skipped with zero decompression!
