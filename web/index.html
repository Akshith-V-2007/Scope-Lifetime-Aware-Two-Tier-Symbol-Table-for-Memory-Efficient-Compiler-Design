1. Monotonic Sorting

Symbols in scope are sorted by name_id:

[ID: 104, x] → [ID: 107, y] → [ID: 112, z]

2. Delta Subtraction

Subsequent IDs encoded as relative increments:

Δ1 = 104, Δ2 = (107 - 104) = 3, Δ3 = (112 - 107) = 5

3. LEB128 Varint & Zigzag

Small deltas and offsets pack into 1–2 bytes:

Byte stream: 0x68 0x03 0x05 ... (60% smaller)
